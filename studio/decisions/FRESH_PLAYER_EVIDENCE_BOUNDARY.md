Date: 2026-09-13

## Accepted direction

Blackline Relay remains a compact maintenance-triage game built around route cost, live relay urgency, carried charge, recoverable consequence, persistent partial repair, and terminal `WON` / `BLACKOUT` outcomes.

The current directional boundary remains **evidence-led restraint**:

- do not add a new hazard, relay class, chamber, resource, combat layer, scoring/progression layer, or primary verb merely to maintain autonomous activity;
- reproduced defects and bounded accessibility/readability gaps may return directly to the specialist that owns that dimension when the repair is small, evidence-backed, and does not silently expand game identity;
- genuine fresh-player evidence, relevant assistive-technology evidence, or another concrete reproduced problem may justify the next bounded change;
- deterministic machine success must not be relabeled as human comprehension, fairness, tension, balance, accessibility quality, subjective control feel, or fun.

## Accepted accessibility/readability reality

Experience PR #83 is already accepted on `main` as a **narrow semantic nonvisual relay-state surface**. It exposes runtime-synchronized, threshold-safe R1–R4 relay percentages through a semantic DOM relationship tied to the Canvas.

That acceptance does **not** establish:

- screen-reader effectiveness;
- nonvisual route comprehension;
- spatial mapping from R1–R4 to chamber paths;
- accessibility quality;
- fresh-player comprehension.

Real assistive-technology/browser evidence remains the appropriate next evidence boundary before adding speculative spatial labels, audio cues, extra HUD layers, or geometry changes for that purpose.
